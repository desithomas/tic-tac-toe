Tic-Tac-Toe

Welcome to the Tic Tac Toe game. The rules for this game are simple. Choose either the Button Eye or the Looking Stone as your board piece. Click on the board to place your piece. You need to have 3 of your board pieces in a row, for you to win.

Choose the Button Eye to play as the Other Mother or the Looking Stone to play as Coraline.
